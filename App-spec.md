# Eternal D Collab

A simple free AI collaboration app.

## Goal

Free real-time chat and tasks.
## First version

- Create a shared workspace and invite someone to join.
- Send and receive messages in a shared chat. New messages appear without refreshing.
- Create tasks, assign them to workspace members, and mark them complete.
- Ask the AI to suggest tasks from a chat discussion. Review the suggestions before adding them to the task list.

## First test

Two people join the same workspace. One sends a message, and the other sees it without refreshing. They ask the AI to suggest a task, add it after reviewing it, and confirm that both people see when it is marked complete.
